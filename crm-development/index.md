# Custom CRM development and workflow automation

We build custom CRMs and automate the work around them: capturing leads from every channel, routing them to the right person, following up on WhatsApp and email, and reporting revenue in one place. Or we connect and extend the CRM you already use.

## What we build

- **Custom CRM software**: A CRM shaped around how you sell: your stages, fields, roles and approvals, on web and mobile, without paying for seats and features you never use.
- **CRM integration and customisation**: Connect and extend the CRM you already use, such as HubSpot, Zoho CRM or Salesforce, with custom fields, workflows and two-way data sync.
- **Lead capture and routing**: Leads from your website, ads, WhatsApp, calls and marketplaces land in one pipeline, de-duplicated and assigned automatically.
- **Sales and marketing automation**: Follow-up sequences, reminders, quotes and proposals triggered by what each lead does, over WhatsApp, email and SMS.
- **Business process automation**: Approvals, onboarding, invoicing and reporting moved from spreadsheets into automated workflows with an audit trail.
- **Dashboards and reporting**: Live pipeline, conversion and revenue dashboards, so sales, marketing and leadership read the same numbers.

## FAQ

**Should we build a custom CRM or use HubSpot, Zoho or Salesforce?**
If an off-the-shelf CRM fits your process with light configuration, we will say so and set it up. A custom CRM pays off when your workflow is unusual, when per-seat pricing gets expensive, or when you need deep integration with your own systems.

**How long does custom CRM development take?**
A focused CRM with the core pipeline, roles and integrations typically reaches production in about 45 days, then grows in two-week releases.

**Can you migrate our data from spreadsheets or another CRM?**
Yes. We clean, de-duplicate and map your contacts, companies and deals, run a test import, and migrate with a rollback plan.

**Can the CRM send WhatsApp messages automatically?**
Yes. Through the official WhatsApp Business Platform the CRM can send approved template messages for follow-ups, reminders and notifications, and log every conversation against the lead.

**Who owns the CRM and the data?**
You do. The code, the database and the cloud accounts are yours, and we document everything so your own team or another vendor can take it over.
