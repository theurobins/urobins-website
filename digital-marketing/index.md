# Digital marketing measured in revenue

Paid ads, social media, content and email, planned around the customers you want and tracked all the way to a sale. We build the landing pages and the tracking ourselves, so every campaign can be traced to leads and revenue.

## What we build

- **Performance marketing and PPC**: Google Search, Performance Max, YouTube and Meta campaigns built around cost per lead and return on ad spend, not clicks.
- **Social media marketing**: Content calendars, creative and community management on Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook and X, plus paid social to reach beyond your followers.
- **Lead generation funnels**: Landing pages, lead magnets, forms and WhatsApp or email follow-up that turn ad clicks into qualified enquiries in your CRM.
- **Content marketing**: Articles, guides, case studies and video scripts that answer buyer questions and serve SEO, sales and social at once.
- **Email and WhatsApp marketing**: Segmented newsletters, nurture sequences and broadcast campaigns, with consent and deliverability handled properly.
- **Analytics and conversion tracking**: Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager, the Meta Conversions API and server-side tracking, so you can trust the numbers you budget on.

## Why Urobins

- **Tracking first**: We fix analytics and conversion tracking before scaling spend, so optimisation decisions rest on real data.
- **Landing pages we build**: Fast, focused pages designed and coded in-house and tested against each other, instead of sending paid traffic to a generic homepage.
- **Revenue, not vanity metrics**: Reports show cost per lead, pipeline and revenue next to impressions and clicks, in a dashboard you can open any time.
- **Connected to sales**: Leads flow straight into your CRM with their source attached, so you see which campaigns produce customers, not just form fills.

## FAQ

**What digital marketing services does Urobins offer?**
Performance marketing on Google and Meta, social media marketing, content marketing, email and WhatsApp marketing, landing pages and lead generation funnels, and analytics and conversion tracking, plus SEO from the same team.

**How much should we spend on ads?**
It depends on your market, margins and goals. We usually start with a test budget large enough to learn from within a few weeks, then scale the campaigns that hit your target cost per lead.

**How soon will paid ads show results?**
Campaigns bring traffic from the first day. Most accounts settle into a stable cost per lead after a few weeks of testing and optimisation.

**Do you create the ad creatives and landing pages?**
Yes. Our designers make the static and video creative and our developers build and test the landing pages, so the whole funnel is in one team’s hands.

**How do you report on marketing performance?**
In a shared dashboard with spend, leads, cost per lead, conversions and revenue, plus a monthly review against your goals.

**Do you work with B2B as well as B2C brands?**
Yes. B2B campaigns lean on LinkedIn, search and content with longer nurture sequences; B2C leans on Meta, Google and fast landing pages. We plan the mix around how your customers buy.
