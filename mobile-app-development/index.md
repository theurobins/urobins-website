# Mobile app development for iOS and Android

We design, build and launch mobile apps for iOS and Android, native in Swift and Kotlin or cross-platform in Flutter, together with the backend, admin panel and analytics they need to run. You test a real build on your own phone every two weeks.

## What we build

- **Cross-platform apps in Flutter**: One codebase for iOS and Android with native performance: the right fit for most startup and business apps that need both stores quickly.
- **Native iOS and Android apps**: Swift and Kotlin builds for apps that lean on the camera, Bluetooth, background location, health data or heavy graphics.
- **App UI and UX design**: User research, flows and a design system in Figma, with clickable prototypes tested before development starts.
- **Backend, APIs and admin panels**: Secure APIs, databases, authentication, push notifications and an admin dashboard, so your team can run the app day to day.
- **Payments, maps and integrations**: In-app payments and subscriptions, maps and live tracking, chat, and connections to your CRM, ERP or existing systems.
- **Launch, support and growth**: App Store and Google Play submission, crash monitoring, analytics, regular updates and app store optimisation after launch.

## FAQ

**How much does it cost to develop a mobile app in India?**
It depends on the features, the platforms and the backend the app needs. After a short discovery call we send a written proposal with a fixed scope for the first release and a timeline, so you know what you are paying for before we start.

**How long does mobile app development take?**
A focused MVP usually reaches production in about 45 days. Larger apps ship in phases, with a new test build on your phone every two weeks.

**Should I choose Flutter or native iOS and Android development?**
For most business and startup apps, Flutter gives you both platforms from one codebase at a lower cost. Native Swift and Kotlin make sense when an app depends heavily on device hardware or platform-specific features. We recommend one after discovery and explain why.

**Do you publish the app on the App Store and Google Play?**
Yes. We prepare the store listings, screenshots and privacy details, handle submission and review, and publish under your own developer accounts, so you own the app.

**Do you provide app maintenance after launch?**
Yes. We monitor crashes and performance, ship updates for new iOS and Android versions, fix issues and keep adding features in regular releases.

**Can you build the backend and admin panel too?**
Yes. We build the API, database, authentication, notifications and an admin dashboard, and host them on secure cloud infrastructure with monitoring and backups.
