# SEO services built on engineering

Rankings start with a site that search engines can crawl, understand and trust. We fix the technical foundations, then grow organic traffic with content and on-page work aimed at the searches your customers actually make, and report it against leads and revenue.

## What we build

- **Technical SEO audits**: Crawling, indexing, site architecture, redirects, canonicals, duplicate content and JavaScript rendering, fixed in order of impact.
- **Core Web Vitals and site speed**: Faster LCP, INP and CLS through image, font, script and server work, measured on real-user data in Search Console.
- **On-page SEO and content**: Keyword research, search intent mapping, page briefs and content that answers real questions better than the pages ranking today.

- **Schema and AI search visibility**: Structured data, clear entity information and an llms.txt file, so search engines and AI assistants can understand and cite your business.

## FAQ

**How long does SEO take to show results?**
Technical fixes can show up within weeks of being crawled. Competitive rankings and steady organic leads usually build over three to six months or more, depending on your site, your competitors and how much useful content you publish.

**Do you guarantee first-page Google rankings?**
No, and be wary of anyone who does: nobody controls Google. We commit to the work, the transparency and the reporting, and we measure success in qualified traffic, leads and revenue.

**What does a technical SEO audit include?**
Crawling and indexing, site architecture, internal links, page speed and Core Web Vitals, mobile usability, structured data, on-page elements, content gaps and backlinks, delivered as a prioritised list of fixes that we can also implement.

**Do you offer local SEO in India?**
Yes. For businesses that serve specific cities we optimise your Google Business Profile, build location pages, clean up citations and set up a steady review process.

**Can you help us appear in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews?**
We make your site easy for AI systems to read and cite: clear facts, structured data, an llms.txt file, fast pages and content that answers questions directly. Nobody can guarantee a mention, but these are the signals those systems rely on.

**How do you report on SEO?**
In a shared dashboard with rankings, organic traffic, conversions and revenue, plus a monthly review of what changed and what comes next.
