# Cloud and DevOps that stays up

We design, migrate and run secure cloud infrastructure on AWS and Google Cloud, with CI/CD pipelines, containers, monitoring and backups in place before your first user signs in, so releases are routine and outages are rare.

## What we build

- **Cloud architecture and consulting**: Scalable, secure designs on AWS, Google Cloud or Firebase, sized for today’s traffic with a clear path to the next stage.
- **Cloud migration**: Move from shared hosting, on-premise servers or another cloud with a tested plan, minimal downtime and a rollback at every step.
- **CI/CD pipelines**: Automated builds, tests and deployments with preview environments, so shipping is a routine merge rather than a risky event.
- **Containers and Kubernetes**: Docker images and Kubernetes or serverless setups that scale with demand and keep every environment identical, laptop to production.
- **Monitoring, backups and security**: Uptime and error monitoring, alerting, automated backups, patching, secrets management and least-privilege access.
- **Managed cloud and cost optimisation**: Ongoing support with agreed response times, plus regular reviews that remove idle resources and right-size your cloud bill.

## Why Urobins

- **A 99.9% uptime target**: Every environment we run has monitoring, alerts and backups configured before go-live, with a written runbook for incidents.
- **Infrastructure as code**: Environments defined in code and versioned, so they can be reviewed, rebuilt and audited instead of configured by hand.
- **Security by default**: Encrypted data, private networks, least-privilege roles and patched images are the starting point, not an upgrade.
- **Your accounts, your keys**: Everything runs in cloud accounts you own, documented so your team can operate it with or without us.

## FAQ

**Which cloud platforms do you work with?**
Mainly AWS, Google Cloud and Firebase, with Cloudflare for DNS, CDN and security. We recommend a platform based on your workload, your team and your budget.

**Can you migrate our website or app to the cloud without downtime?**
We plan migrations to keep downtime to minutes or less: a rehearsal on a copy, data sync, a scheduled cut-over and a rollback plan if anything looks wrong.

**What does managed cloud support include?**
Monitoring and alerting, security patching, backups and restore tests, scaling, and incident response with agreed response times and a 99.9% uptime target.

**Can you reduce our cloud costs?**
Usually, yes. We look for idle and oversized resources, storage and data-transfer waste, missing committed-use discounts and architecture changes, then report the savings.

**Do you set up CI/CD for existing projects?**
Yes. We add automated builds, tests and deployments to your existing repositories, with preview environments and safe rollbacks.
