## How we work

1. **Discover**: A technical audit of your product, users and systems, then the architecture and a roadmap you can hold us to. A fixed-scope first sprint: working software inside the first two weeks, not a deck.
2. **Build**: Design and engineering move together: UX, front end, back end and QA, shipped in two-week releases you can click through. Production-ready in about 45 days for a typical MVP.
3. **Launch**: Cloud infrastructure, monitoring, backups and a go-live checklist, so launch day is the quietest day of the project.
4. **Scale**: SEO, performance marketing and CRM automation turn a launched product into a growing business, reported against revenue.
