FAQ

How long does a typical project take?

Most MVPs launch in about 45 days. Larger platforms ship in phased releases, with a working build to review every two weeks.

Do you work with existing codebases?

Yes. We start with a technical audit, then improve, migrate, or extend what you already have instead of rebuilding by default.

What does cloud support include after launch?

Monitoring, security patching, backups, and scaling, with a 99.9% uptime target and agreed response times.

How do you report on SEO and marketing?

You get a shared dashboard with rankings, traffic, conversions, and spend, plus a monthly review against your revenue goals.
