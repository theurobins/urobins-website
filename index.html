FAQ

What services does Urobins offer?

Web development, mobile app development, custom CRM development and automation, cloud and DevOps, SEO, and digital marketing, all from one in-house team.

Where is Urobins based?

Urobins is a remote-first agency in India. We work with startups and businesses across India and worldwide, over video calls, WhatsApp and a shared project channel.

How long does a typical project take?

Most MVPs launch in about 45 days. Larger platforms ship in phased releases, with a working build to review every two weeks.

How much does a website or app cost?

It depends on scope, so we do not publish a price list. Tell us what you are building and we reply within one business day with next steps and a rough timeline; a written proposal follows a short discovery call.

Do you work with existing codebases?

Yes. We start with a technical audit, then improve, migrate, or extend what you already have instead of rebuilding by default.

What does cloud support include after launch?

Monitoring, security patching, backups, and scaling, with a 99.9% uptime target and agreed response times.

How do you report on SEO and marketing?

You get a shared dashboard with rankings, traffic, conversions, and spend, plus a monthly review against your revenue goals.
