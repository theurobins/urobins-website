Mathura and Vrindavan are busy local markets: residents, pilgrims and tourists searching every day for hotels and dharamshalas, restaurants and sweet shops, temples tours and taxis, hospitals and clinics, schools, plots and flats. Most of those searches end on Google Maps. Our regional office is in Vrindavan, so this is a market we know first-hand. Here is how a local business gets found.

## How Google ranks local businesses

Google says local results are based mainly on three things:

1. **Relevance:** how well your profile matches the search.
2. **Distance:** how far you are from the searcher, or the place they named.
3. **Prominence:** how well known you are, from reviews, links, mentions and your overall web presence.

You cannot move your shop closer to every customer, but you can improve relevance and prominence a lot.

## Step 1: Claim and complete your Google Business Profile

- **Verify** the profile. An unverified profile does not show publicly.
- Use your **real business name** only. Adding keywords such as “Best Hotel Vrindavan” to the name breaks Google's guidelines and risks suspension.
- Choose the most **specific primary category** (for example “Vegetarian restaurant”, not just “Restaurant”), plus relevant secondary categories.
- Add **hours** (including festival hours), phone, website, services or menu, and attributes such as wheelchair access.
- Upload real **photos**: storefront, interior, team, products. Add new ones regularly.
- Use **posts** for offers and events, such as Janmashtami or Holi specials.
- Answer common **questions** in your description and on your website.

**Address rules:** list an address only if customers visit you or staff work there during business hours. If you go to customers, as taxi services, tour guides and home services do, hide the address and set a service area. Virtual offices are not allowed.

## Step 2: Build a steady flow of reviews

- Ask every satisfied customer, in person, with a short link or QR code at the counter.
- Reply to every review politely, including negative ones. Future customers read the replies.
- Never buy reviews or offer rewards for positive ones; Google removes them and can penalise the profile.

## Step 3: Keep your name, address and phone identical everywhere

Your business name, address and phone (NAP) should match exactly on your website, Google Business Profile, Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, Facebook, Instagram and any industry directories. Inconsistent details confuse Google and customers.

## Step 4: Give your website local pages

- A page for each main service, mentioning the areas you serve: Vrindavan, Mathura, Govardhan, Barsana, Gokul, as relevant.
- Your **address, phone and a map** on the contact page.
- **LocalBusiness structured data** with the same NAP as your profile.
- **Fast mobile pages**: most local searches happen on phones, often on mobile data. See [Core Web Vitals explained](/blog/core-web-vitals-explained/).

## Step 5: Write for how people actually search

Local searches here mix languages: “hotel near Banke Bihari temple”, “वृंदावन में होटल”, “Mathura peda shop”, “plot in Vrindavan”. Use the words customers use, in Hindi as well as English where it fits. If you publish full Hindi pages, mark them up properly with hreflang so Google shows the right version.

## Step 6: Earn local mentions

Mentions and links from local news sites, tourism portals, temple and event pages, associations and partner businesses build prominence. Sponsoring or taking part in local events often earns them naturally.

## Common mistakes we see

- Keywords stuffed into the business name.
- Several profiles for one business, or profiles at addresses where nobody works.
- Old phone numbers and hours, especially around festivals.
- No replies to reviews.
- A website with no address, no phone and nothing to show it is local.

## Quick checklist

- [ ] Profile verified, primary category precise
- [ ] Hours, photos, services and website complete
- [ ] Review link shared with every customer; every review answered
- [ ] Same name, address and phone on every listing
- [ ] Website with service pages, contact details and LocalBusiness markup
- [ ] Content in the language customers search in
