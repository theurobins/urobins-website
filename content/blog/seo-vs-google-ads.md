When the marketing budget is limited, the first question is where to put it: Google Ads, which you pay for per click, or SEO, which earns free clicks over time. Both put you in front of people searching for what you sell. They work on different clocks.

## How each one works

**Google Ads** shows your ad at the top of search results for keywords you choose. You pay when someone clicks. Traffic starts as soon as campaigns are approved, and stops when you stop paying.

**SEO** (search engine optimisation) earns your pages a place in the organic results below the ads. You do not pay per click, but you invest in a fast website, useful content and authority, and results build over months.

## Side by side

| | Google Ads | SEO |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Speed | Traffic from day one | Usually 3–6 months or more |
| Cost model | Pay per click, ongoing | Investment in content and site, compounding |
| When you stop | Traffic stops | Traffic continues, declining slowly |
| Control | Choose exact keywords, locations, times | Influence, not control |
| Trust | Clearly marked as ads | Many people trust organic results more |
| Best for | Immediate leads, testing, seasonal offers | Long-term, steady growth |

## When to start with Google Ads

- You need **enquiries this month**.
- You are **testing** a new service, location or price and want fast feedback.
- Your offer is **seasonal**, such as festival offers, admissions or tourist season.
- Your website is new and has **no organic visibility yet**. See [how long indexing takes](/blog/get-new-website-indexed-google/).

## When to prioritise SEO

- You are in it for the **long term** and want the cost per lead to fall.
- Customers **research before buying**, which favours guides and comparisons.
- Click prices in your industry are **high**.
- You serve a **local market** where Google Maps rankings drive calls. See our [local SEO guide](/blog/local-seo-mathura-vrindavan/).

## Why most businesses should do both

1. **Ads find the winners.** Within weeks, ads show which keywords turn into enquiries, not just clicks.
2. **SEO targets the winners.** Build pages and articles for those proven keywords.
3. **Ads cover the gap** while SEO grows, then shift to keywords where organic rankings are hardest.
4. **Both build the brand.** People who see you in ads and organic results remember the name and search for it.

## Fix tracking before you spend

Whichever you choose, measure **leads and customers**, not just clicks:

- Google Analytics 4 with conversion events for forms, calls and WhatsApp clicks.
- Google Ads conversion tracking, importing real outcomes where possible.
- Lead source recorded in your CRM, so you can see which channel produced paying customers.

Without this, you will judge channels on clicks, and clicks are not customers.

## A starter plan for a small business

- **Month 1:** fix tracking, launch focused search campaigns on high-intent keywords, set up and verify the Google Business Profile, fix technical SEO basics.
- **Months 2–3:** cut keywords that do not convert, build landing pages for those that do, publish content answering customers' top questions.
- **Months 4–6:** grow content and local SEO, rebalance budget towards the channel with the lowest cost per customer.
