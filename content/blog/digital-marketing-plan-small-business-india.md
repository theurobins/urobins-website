Small businesses are told to be everywhere: Instagram, YouTube, Google, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, ads, influencers, email. With limited time and budget, that usually means doing everything badly. A better plan starts with the basics customers check before they buy, then adds one channel at a time.

## Step 1: The foundations (week 1–2)

Before spending on ads, make sure the places people check are ready:

- **Google Business Profile:** verified, with categories, hours, photos and reviews. For local businesses this is often the single most valuable listing. See our [local SEO guide](/blog/local-seo-mathura-vrindavan/).
- **A fast mobile website:** clear services, prices or price guidance where possible, location, and one obvious way to contact you. See [what a website costs](/blog/website-cost-india/).
- **WhatsApp:** a business number that is answered quickly, with a greeting and quick replies. As you grow, connect it to a CRM; see the [WhatsApp Business API guide](/blog/whatsapp-business-api-guide/).
- **Consistent details:** the same name, address, phone and email everywhere.

## Step 2: Tracking (week 2)

Set up Google Analytics 4 with conversion events for form submissions, calls and WhatsApp clicks. Record the source of every lead (“How did you hear about us?”) in your CRM or register. This is what lets you double down on what works.

## Step 3: Choose one or two channels (month 1)

| If your customers… | Start with |
| --- | --- |
| Search for what you sell (“CA near me”, “AC repair Mathura”) | **Google Search Ads + SEO** |
| Buy on impulse or visually (food, fashion, decor, travel) | **Instagram and Meta ads** |
| Are other businesses | **LinkedIn + Google Search** |
| Need a product explained | **YouTube + search** |
| Are existing customers who could return | **WhatsApp and email** |

Pick the one or two that match. You can add more once the first ones work.

## Step 4: Content that answers questions (ongoing)

Write down the 20 questions customers ask before buying, then answer them: on your website, in short videos and in social posts. Content that answers real questions works for SEO, for AI answers (see [SEO, AEO and GEO explained](/blog/what-is-geo-aeo-ai-search/)) and for sales conversations.

## Step 5: Paid campaigns, carefully (month 1–3)

- Start **small and focused**: one offer, one audience, one landing page.
- Send ad traffic to a **page about that offer**, not your home page.
- Judge campaigns on **cost per enquiry and per sale**.
- Turn off what does not work after a fair test, and scale what does.
- See [SEO vs Google Ads](/blog/seo-vs-google-ads/) for choosing between them.

## Step 6: Keep customers coming back

Your existing customers are your cheapest growth. With their consent, send useful WhatsApp or email updates: reorder reminders, festival offers, new arrivals, service reminders. Ask for reviews and referrals.

## A simple monthly rhythm

- **Weekly:** post consistently on your chosen channel; reply to every message and review.
- **Monthly:** review enquiries and sales by source; adjust budgets; publish two or three helpful articles or videos.
- **Quarterly:** refresh offers and creatives; check your website speed and Google Business Profile.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Boosting posts with no clear goal or audience.
- Measuring followers and likes instead of enquiries.
- Sending ads to a slow or generic home page.
- Buying followers, reviews or contact lists.
- Starting five channels at once and abandoning them all.
